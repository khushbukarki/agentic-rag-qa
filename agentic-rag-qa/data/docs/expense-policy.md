# Harbourview Logistics — Expense Policy

This is a fictional sample document used to demonstrate the system.

## Vehicle mileage

Staff who use a private vehicle for business travel are reimbursed at $0.95 per kilometre. Travel between home and your usual workplace is not reimbursable. Record the start point, destination and purpose of each trip.

## Meals and accommodation

Meal allowances are capped at $40 per day for domestic travel and $70 per day for international travel. Alcohol is not reimbursable. Accommodation must be booked through the travel desk and should not exceed $220 per night in Auckland or Wellington and $180 per night elsewhere in New Zealand.

## Submitting claims

All claims must be submitted in ExpenseHub within 30 days of the expense. Receipts are required for any single item over $50. Claims submitted late may be declined.

## Approval limits

Your manager can approve claims up to $2,000. Claims above $2,000 also require approval from the Finance Director. Approved claims are paid in the next fortnightly pay run.
