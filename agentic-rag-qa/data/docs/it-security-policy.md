# Harbourview Logistics — IT Security Policy

This is a fictional sample document used to demonstrate the system.

## Access and authentication

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for email, VPN and all remote access to company systems. Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Passwords only need to be changed if you suspect they have been compromised, not on a fixed schedule.

## Devices

All company laptops are encrypted with BitLocker and must be locked when unattended. A lost or stolen device must be reported to the IT Service Desk (extension 4400) within 24 hours so it can be remotely wiped.

## Phishing and incidents

Report suspicious emails using the Report Phish button in Outlook. Do not click links or open attachments in a message you suspect is phishing. If you have already clicked a link, contact the IT Service Desk immediately and change your password.

## Software

Only software from the approved Company Portal may be installed on company devices. Requests for new software go through the IT Service Desk and are reviewed by the security team within 5 working days.
