# Harbourview Logistics — Leave Policy

This is a fictional sample document used to demonstrate the system.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 20 days of annual leave per year, accrued each pay period. After five years of continuous service, the entitlement increases to 25 days per year. Part-time employees accrue annual leave pro rata based on their contracted hours.

Up to 5 days of unused annual leave may be carried over into the next leave year. Any balance above 5 days must be used by 31 March or it will be paid out at the employee's ordinary rate.

## Requesting leave

Requests for more than 3 consecutive days of leave must be submitted in HRPortal at least two weeks in advance. Shorter requests need at least 48 hours' notice. Your manager will approve or decline within 3 working days.

## Sick leave

Employees become entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per year after six months of service. Unused sick leave carries over up to a maximum balance of 20 days. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

## Public holidays

If you work on a public holiday, you are paid time and a half for the hours worked and receive an alternative paid day off to use at a later date.
